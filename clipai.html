ClipAI

ClipAI is an AI-powered UGC video editing app concept I designed and built end-to-end, from initial product thinking through a live working prototype. It addresses a critical friction point for creators: the agonizing hours spent manually cutting out silences, filler words, and syncing captions. By orchestrating a robust AI pipeline under the hood, ClipAI auto-edits raw talking-head footage into highly engaging shorts within seconds.

Frictionless Mobile Capture

The mobile interface operates as the creator's immediate sandbox. The primary objective is to drastically lower the activation energy required to start creating. Built using minimalist glass panes and massive hit-targets, users can jump into recording or pull content directly from the cloud instantly. The UX abstracts away complex timelines into simple, digestible draft cards, keeping the cognitive load entirely on the creative process.

Advanced AI Web Editor

For creators needing granular control, I designed a scalable desktop web editor environment. This interface introduces a 'Smart Timeline' that visually demarcates AI-generated cuts from user-retained footage. It features a sophisticated AI-powered reporting panel that grades the video's 'Hook Strength', scores its trend alignment (e.g. 87/100), and auto-suggests precise timestamp overlays, empowering the creator with data-driven editing decisions.

High-Conversion Positioning

To communicate the core value proposition efficiently, I engineered a highly-converting landing page. Architected around the compelling thesis 'record it. we edit it.', the page immediately builds trust. It utilizes a strategic bento-box feature breakdown, highlighting auto-captions, trending music match, and multi-clip threading, and culminates in an aggressive, gamified waitlist flow.

The Functional Logic Prototype

Design means nothing without execution. I developed a fully functional software prototype powered by Anthropic's Claude API, deployed on Vercel. By passing raw transcription arrays through a rigid JSON schema, the Large Language Model intelligently processes, grades, and physically truncates video block arrays in real-time, proving the technical viability of the product thesis.
